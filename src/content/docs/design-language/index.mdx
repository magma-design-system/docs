## What the design language is

A component decides how it looks. It does not decide how far it stands from the next one, which
of three actions weighs more, how a title sits above its content, when a group needs a surface of
its own or how wide a form gets. The design language decides those questions, the same way in
every product, so that two screens made by two teams read as one product.

Each domain is one of those questions: rhythm and density for space, actions for the weight of
what the person can do, titles, surfaces, widths and alignment. The
[building blocks](../blocks/) and the [patterns](../patterns/) follow these rules, and the
screens of a product follow them too.

## Reading a domain

A domain reads like a pattern. It starts from the context of an element, where it stands and
what stands around it: each combination is a **case**, which says what to use. The stage at the
top of the page runs the cases one at a time, and the rules that hold everywhere (**Always
true**) where Magma shows them, with a line on what to try. **Situations** are real screens, each
with the case it falls in; **Decisions** says when each rule was decided and why.

## With an AI agent

An agent reads the same files. It asks the Magma MCP server for the rule of its context with
`get_pattern`, or for a whole domain with `get_guide` and its id (`rhythm`). How to set the agent
up is in [Agentic development](../ai/).

## Where they come from

The domains ship with Magma, in the package `@maggioli-design-system/magma`, beside the
patterns. Each page here is generated from the file of its domain, and it is written in English.
The decisions they rest on, the principles, accessibility, color and type, are in
[Foundation](../foundation/).
