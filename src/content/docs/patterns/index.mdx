A component solves one part of a screen; people come to a product to get something done. A
pattern is the solution to a task that the products of Maggioli Group meet again and again -
confirming an action, searching a list, filling in a form - put together from Magma components
and [building blocks](../blocks/), with the decisions the components cannot take on their own:
what follows an action, what the page shows when nothing matches, what goes into the address,
where the focus goes, what a screen reader hears.

The heart of a pattern is its behavior, so its size varies. It can be as small as a button that
opens a dialog: what the dialog asks, where the focus goes when it closes, which message follows.
Or as large as a whole page, such as a list to search and narrow down. A component is imported
ready to use; a pattern is applied, following its guide and its code.

## What a pattern page holds

Every pattern will have the same parts: the pattern running, when to use it, its anatomy, the
components it is made of, its behavior, its accessibility, its texts, the mistakes to avoid and,
last, its code in plain HTML, React and Angular. The code will be complete: copy it into a
project where Magma is installed, replace the demo data with your back end, and it runs.

## The first ones

- **List with filters**: records to search, narrow down, sort and page through.
- **Record detail**: one record, what can be done with it and its history.
- **Form and validation**: data to fill in, checked as people type and when they send it.
- **Empty and error states**: what a screen shows when there is nothing yet, or something
  went wrong.

The Pattern and Antipattern pages of each [component](../components/) already show how to use
that component on its own. This section will show how components and building blocks work
together to complete a task.
