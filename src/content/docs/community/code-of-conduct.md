---
title: Code of conduct
description: 'The code of conduct of the Magma community: what is expected, what is not accepted and how to report it.'
---

The Magma community follows a code of conduct, adapted from the Contributor Covenant 2.1. It
holds in every space of the community, such as the issues, pull requests and discussions of
the Magma repository, and for whoever represents the community in public.

- **What is expected**: empathy and kindness, respect for opinions and experiences other
  than yours, feedback given and taken constructively, owning your mistakes and apologizing
  to those affected, caring for the good of the community and not only your own.
- **What is not accepted**: sexualized language or images and unwanted advances, insults,
  trolling and personal or political attacks, harassment in public or in private,
  publishing someone's private information without their permission, and any other
  behavior out of place in a professional setting.
- **Reports**: write to the community leaders, at the address in the code of conduct. They
  review every report promptly and fairly, and protect the privacy of whoever reports.
  Depending on the harm done, the consequence goes from a private warning to a temporary or
  permanent ban from the community.

The full text is in the
[code of conduct of the Magma repository](https://github.com/magma-design-system/magma/blob/dev/.github/CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md).
