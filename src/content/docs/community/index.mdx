Where to ask about Magma, follow how it changes, try its components and shape the theme of your product:

<Channels>
<Channel
  name="Stack Overflow"
  kind="Questions and answers"
  icon="mdi/stack-overflow"
  variant="orange"
  href="https://stackoverflowteams.com/c/magma-design-system/questions"
  action="Open Stack Overflow"
>

The questions and answers about Magma, in a Stack Overflow for Teams space of its own. Ask
how to do something with Magma, or search first: someone may have asked already, and every
answer stays there for whoever comes next. To read and to ask, join the team with a Stack
Overflow account.

</Channel>
<Channel
  name="GitHub"
  kind="Pull requests"
  icon="mdi/github"
  variant="primary"
  href="https://github.com/magma-design-system/magma/pulls"
  action="Open the pull requests"
>

The changes to Magma in the works. Each pull request is a proposed change, with its
discussion, its review and its checks, until it is merged and released. Follow what is
coming, comment on a change, or propose your own.

</Channel>
<Channel
  name="Storybook"
  kind="Components in action"
  icon="mi/baseline/auto-stories"
  variant="orchid"
  href="https://magma-design-system.github.io/magma/storybook/?path=/story/common-tests-semantic-surfaces--example-page"
  action="Open Storybook"
>

Magma's workshop, where every component shows its variants and states: change its props
from the controls, see the events it sends and check its accessibility. It also holds the
type scale, the icons and the test pages of colors and surfaces. The link opens Acme
Console, an application screen built with Magma followed by the whole component library,
in light and dark mode.

</Channel>
<Channel
  name="Magma Studio"
  kind="Themes"
  icon="mi/baseline/palette"
  variant="violet"
  href={STUDIO}
  action="Open Magma Studio"
>

Where a product shapes its theme, in the browser and with nothing to install. It starts
from the configuration Magma is built with, or from one of yours, and you change it where
the change belongs, from one color to the whole product: set how much contrast each role of
the text needs, and the studio picks the colors that meet it, in every theme and in light and
dark mode, judging every pair while you edit. See the theme on the screen of a product, then
download the configuration, the tokens and the Figma tokens.

</Channel>
</Channels>
