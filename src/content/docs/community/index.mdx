The questions and answers about Magma, in a Stack Overflow for Teams space of its own. Ask

Overflow account.

The changes to Magma in the works. Each pull request is a proposed change, with its
discussion, its review and its checks, until it is merged and released. Follow what is
coming, comment on a change, or propose your own.

</Channel>

Magma's workshop, where every component shows its variants and states: change its props

in light and dark mode.
