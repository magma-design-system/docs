Every Magma package ships a `CHANGELOG.md` with its releases. This page shows them from Magma
2.0.0 on, newest first: each version holds the same entries as its release on GitHub, grouped
by kind of change. An entry marked **Breaking** may ask for a change in your project: read it
before you update, together with [Versions and migrations](../development/versions/).

## Magma

The components, `@maggioli-design-system/magma`. `magma-react` and `magma-angular` are released
together with it, at the same version, with the same entries.

<Changelog pkg="magma" />

## Styles

The CSS of Magma, `@maggioli-design-system/styles`: the semantic tokens, the Tailwind theme and
the utilities.

<Changelog pkg="styles" />

## Design tokens

The raw values of the design decisions, `@maggioli-design-system/design-tokens`: color ramps,
type scale, spacing, shadows, motion.

<Changelog pkg="design-tokens" />

## Identity

Logos, avatars and the other brand assets, `@maggioli-design-system/identity`.

<Changelog pkg="identity" />
