Name the role, not the color. A semantic role (`--magma-*`) says what an element is, a
raised card, muted text, the primary action, and resolves to a color for the mode, the
named theme and the contrast preference of the page. A palette step only flips with the
mode: it stays grey under a tinted theme and gains no contrast when the user asks for
more.

## Two ways to write a role

Every role is a custom property holding rgb channels, and a Tailwind color.

```css
.panel {
  background: rgb(var(--magma-surface-raised));
  color: rgb(var(--magma-text-default));
  border: 1px solid rgb(var(--magma-border-muted));
}
```

```html
<section class="bg-surface-raised text-fg-default border border-border-muted">
  <p class="text-fg-muted">Secondary text</p>
</section>
```

In Tailwind the text roles are `fg-*`, so that no class reads `text-text-*`:
`--magma-text-muted` is `text-fg-muted`. Every other role keeps its name. The roles come
from `styles/dist/css/semantic.css`, their Tailwind colors from
`styles/dist/tailwind/theme.css`; an opacity modifier works as usual
(`bg-surface-raised/80`).

## Neutral roles

The backgrounds, text and borders of everything that is neither an action nor a state. A
named theme retints all of them at once.

### Surfaces

Five levels, chosen by where the element sits, not by the shade: `default` is the page,
`sunken` is recessed into it (wells, code blocks, tracks), `muted` groups on the page plane
(zebra rows), `raised` is lifted off it (cards, panels, sticky bars), `overlay` floats
above everything (modals, dropdowns, popovers). In light mode the levels above the page are
close, so give `raised` and `overlay` a shadow too, as the components do.

### Wash

A neutral pill, chip or hover, by how marked it is. `wash-soft` carries the whole text
ladder, `wash-base` only `text-default`, `wash-strong` only icons.

<TokenTable tokens="magma.wash" />

### Text

`text-muted` is for secondary text that is still essential, `text-subtle` only for what
the user can skip. Every text role is legible on all five surfaces in both modes.

<TokenTable tokens="magma.text" />

### Borders

`border-muted` is decorative (dividers, table grids), `border-default` outlines what
works (inputs, cards), `border-strong` conveys a state, `border-focus` follows the accent.

<TokenTable tokens="magma.border" />

### Other neutral roles

The inverse chip and its ink, the ink on the modal scrim, the selection, and the color
of a projected shadow, to compose with your own geometry and alpha.
`--magma-neutral-emphasis` and `--magma-neutral-on-emphasis` are deprecated aliases of
`--magma-surface-inverse` and `--magma-on-inverse`.

## Accents

Two accents with the same nine roles: `accent`, the action color of the brand, and
`accent-ai`, for features driven by AI. `-fg` is ink on a neutral surface, `-on-emphasis`
is ink on the solid fill: swapping them is the quickest way to break contrast.

## Status

Info, success, warning and danger publish the same vocabulary: washes with their text,
borders, a solid fill with its ink, and three shortcuts at the default prominence
(`-surface`, `-fg`, `-border`). The danger role uses the `status-error` palette. A named
theme never retints a status; the contrast preference promotes its text.

## Pairing rules

- **`-fg` on a neutral surface, `-on-emphasis` on the fill.**
- **Text on a wash is bounded**: `wash-soft` takes the whole text ladder, `wash-base` only
  `text-default`, `wash-strong` icons only. The same holds for every status.
- **Hierarchy without losing contrast**: use weight, size and spacing, and soften the
  chrome (`border-muted`, zebra on `surface-muted`) rather than the text.
- **Elevation is a role**: page `surface-default`, card `surface-raised`, floating layer
  `surface-overlay`, well `surface-sunken`. A grey that has to look marked in both modes is
  a wash, not a surface.

These are the pairs Magma measures for contrast; any other combination is not measured.

## Palettes

The primitives the roles point at, from `01` to `10`. Paint with them only when the color
is data: a category, a tag, a color the user picked (`label-*`), a brand (`brand-*`). The
strips follow the mode of this page; a click on a swatch copies its custom property.

### Tones

The neutral families. `neutral` is the grey of the default theme; `porcelain` and `bisque`
back the `cool` and `warm` themes: switch those on with `data-theme-name`, do not paint
with them by hand. `seed` is the paper of the mode, white in light and black in dark.
