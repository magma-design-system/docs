## Layout

- The parts always come in the same order: back, overline, title with status, description,
  actions. A part that is missing leaves no room behind.
- The title takes the size of `typography="h3"` on every page, whatever its level: the size
  comes from `typography`, the level from `tag`.
- The actions stand at the right of the text from `lg`, aligned to its top, and under it on a
  narrower screen. The text takes the room that is left (`min-w-0`), so a long title wraps
  instead of pushing the actions out.
- The status follows the title on the same line, and goes under it when there is no room.
- Three actions at most. More belong to a menu or to the parts of the page they act on, such
  as the rows of a table.

## Accessibility

- The title is the only `h1` of the page, and the first heading a screen reader finds.
- When an action removes the button that ran it, the focus goes to the title, which takes it
  with `tabindex="-1"` and shows no ring.
- The status is the text of the badge, not only its color.
- The back link is a link, named after the page it leads to: a screen reader announces where
  it goes.

## Content

- Name the page after what it holds, in the plural for a list ("Requests"), after the record
  for a record ("Change of residence").
- The description is one sentence, without a full stop when it is a list of facts.
- The labels of the actions start with a verb and name the object when it is not obvious:
  "Close the request", "Add user".
