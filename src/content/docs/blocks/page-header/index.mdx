The first thing people read on a page: where they are, what the page holds and what they can do
there. It comes back in every pattern written so far - the [list with
filters](../../patterns/list-with-filters/), the [record detail](../../patterns/record-detail/),
the users of [Messages](../../patterns/messages/) - and each one had built it a little
differently. Written once, it reads the same on every page of a product.

<PatternPreview id="page-header" kind="blocks" />

## When to use it

At the top of every page of an application, once per page: its title is the `h1` of the page.
Not for the sections inside a page, which have a heading of their own, nor for a dialog, whose
title is part of `mds-modal`.

## Anatomy

1. **Back** (optional): a link to the page this one belongs to, named as that page names
   itself: "Requests", not "Back". Only when the page has a page above it, such as a record and
   its list.
2. **Overline** (optional): the number or the kind of the record, small, above the title.
3. **Title**: the name of the page, or of the record it shows.
4. **Status** (optional): the status of the record, as a badge next to the title.
5. **Description** (optional): one sentence - what the page holds, or the facts of the record.
6. **Actions** (optional): what can be done with the whole page or record, the main one last.

From `lg` the actions stand at the right of the text, aligned to its top; on a narrower screen
they go under it, and wrap when they do not fit on one line.
