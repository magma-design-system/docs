In Maggioli's products color is information before it is decoration: it says what can be
pressed, what went wrong, what belongs together. This page says which color to use for what;
the tokens, their values and the pairs they are measured in are in
[Tokens: Color](../../tokens/color/).

## Every color has one meaning

Each family of colors has a job, and keeps it everywhere: once red means danger, it cannot
also be the accent of a banner in the same product. That is why Magma names colors by what
they do (a surface, a text, the accent, danger) and not by their hue: the name says the
meaning, and the value can change with the brand, the mode and the contrast preference.

## The interface is neutral

Surfaces, text and borders are greys: the neutral of the default theme, or one of the two
tinted ones, cool and warm, that a product can pick as its theme. Most of a screen is
neutral, so that the few colored elements stand out, and mean something.

## One accent for actions

The accent is the action color of the brand: the primary button, the link, the selected
item, the focus. A view has one primary action; when everything is in the accent, nothing
stands out.

## A second accent for AI

The features driven by artificial intelligence have their own accent, so that users can tell
at a glance what an AI proposes from what the product does. It goes with `mds-policy-ai`,
which marks the content an AI produced and links to the policy that governs it.

## Four states

- **Info**: something useful to know, with nothing to do. For example, a new version of the
  product, the last login.
- **Success**: something went as expected. For example, a document saved, a payment
  registered.
- **Warning**: something that will become a problem if it is ignored. For example, a license
  about to expire, a maintenance window.
- **Danger**: something went wrong, or is about to happen and cannot be undone. For example,
  a field that is not valid, deleting a record.

The states do not change with the theme or the brand: a warning looks like a warning in every
product of the group.

## Labels and brands are data

Label colors are for the categories users create or recognize: tags, folders, the subject
areas of an editorial product. Brand colors identify a company or a product of the group, in
its logo and in its editorial pages. Neither is used to paint the interface, nor to tell a
state.

<mds-usage variant="do">

## A ladder of contrast

Every palette has ten steps, from `01`, the most contrasted with the page, to `10`, the
closest to it. The steps are generated from contrast targets rather than from lightness, with
Adobe's Leonardo. So palettes of the same kind are interchangeable: the same step of info and
of success has the same contrast, and swapping one for the other does not change what can be
read. Dark mode is the same ladder on a dark page.

## Never color alone

A state or a category is never told by color only: it also has a text, an icon or a shape.
More in [Accessibility](../accessibility/#never-color-alone).

## One product at a time

Many products of the group have colors of their own, older than Magma. They do not have to
replace them in one go: they paint the new screens with the color roles, and move the others
when they get to them. Consistency between the products grows one release at a time.
