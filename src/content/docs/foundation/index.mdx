The components, the tokens and the icons of Magma are the result of a few decisions taken
before them: who the products are for, what they must never get wrong, how they look and how
they speak. This section writes those decisions down, so that a team can apply them where no
component reaches, and argue with them when they no longer fit.

One set of [principles](principles/) holds them together. Every other page is a domain of
decisions: each decision says what it serves, and none adds principles of its own.
